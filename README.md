### <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/WFZvB7VIXBgiz3oDXE/giphy.gif" width="28"/> About Me

I build systems that sit at the boundary between **research and production** — taking ideas from papers on attention mechanisms and reinforcement learning and shaping them into pipelines that actually ship. My work centers on **LLM engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, and applied deep learning**, with a growing focus on **agentic architectures** that can plan, use tools, and reason over multi-step tasks.

I'm drawn to problems where the constraint isn't "can this work" but **"can this work reliably, at scale, under real data"** — that's where most ML projects actually fail, and where I spend most of my time.
